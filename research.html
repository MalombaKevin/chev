Research areas

Genomic Instability

Epigenetic Clocks

Mitochondrial Health

Energy production, oxidative stress and the decline of mitochondrial function in aging tissue.

🔥

Inflammaging

Brain Aging

Neurodegeneration, cognitive resilience and the aging nervous system.

🦴

Stem Cell Exhaustion

Why tissues lose regenerative capacity and how to restore it.
