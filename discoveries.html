Discoveries

A timeline of the breakthroughs that shaped the field.

DiscoveriesGenes & PathwaysMoleculesTechnologiesAging Topics
1935

Caloric restriction extends lifespan

Rats on restricted diets lived significantly longer — the first hint that aging is modifiable.

1961

The Hayflick limit

Normal human cells divide a limited number of times before entering senescence.

1993

Single-gene lifespan extension

Mutations in the daf-2 gene doubled lifespan in C. elegans worms.

2006

Cellular reprogramming

Four transcription factors can turn adult cells back into pluripotent stem cells.

2009

Rapamycin extends mouse lifespan

The first drug shown to extend lifespan in mammals, even when started late in life.

2013

Epigenetic clocks

DNA methylation patterns can predict chronological and biological age with high accuracy.

2015

First senolytics

Drugs that selectively kill senescent cells improved health in aged mice.
