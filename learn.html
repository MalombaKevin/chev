How science is tackling aging

The most common approaches researchers are pursuing to slow, stop or reverse biological aging.

🧹

Senolytics

Drugs that clear senescent "zombie" cells, which build up with age and drive inflammation.

🔄

Epigenetic Reprogramming

Resetting the chemical marks on DNA to return cells to a more youthful state.

🍽️

Caloric Restriction & Fasting

Eating less, or within time windows, to switch on longevity pathways like AMPK and sirtuins.

💊

Geroprotective Drugs

Existing medicines such as rapamycin and metformin tested for their ability to slow aging.

🧫

Stem Cells & Regeneration

Replacing or rejuvenating stem cells so aged tissues can repair themselves again.

🧬

Gene Therapy

Delivering or editing genes to counter age-related decline in specific tissues.

⏱️

Biomarkers & Aging Clocks

Measuring biological age so researchers can tell whether an intervention is working.

🔋

Mitochondrial Medicine

Restoring cellular energy with NAD+ boosters and mitochondria-targeted therapies.

🛡️

Immunology & Inflammaging

Rejuvenating the aging immune system and calming chronic low-grade inflammation.

🏃

Exercise Science

The best-proven intervention — how physical activity slows aging in muscle, heart and brain.

🦠

Microbiome

How gut bacteria shift with age and influence metabolism, immunity and cognition.

🧠

Neuroscience of Aging

Protecting memory and cognition against neurodegenerative disease.
