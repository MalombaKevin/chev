Molecules

Compounds being studied for their effect on aging.

DiscoveriesGenes & PathwaysMoleculesTechnologiesAging Topics
mTOR inhibitorAnimal evidence strong

Rapamycin

Extends lifespan in mice; human trials are exploring immune and healthspan benefits.

Diabetes drugHuman trials planned

Metformin

Acts on metabolism; being investigated as a potential aging-targeting drug.

Metabolic cofactorEarly human trials

NAD+ precursors (NR, NMN)

NAD+ declines with age; precursors aim to restore levels.

SenolyticPilot human studies

Dasatinib + Quercetin

A combination that clears senescent cells in animal models.

PolyamineObservational data

Spermidine

Induces autophagy; found in foods like wheat germ and aged cheese.

Flavonoid senolyticEarly human trials

Fisetin

A plant compound with senolytic activity in mice.

Educational content only. None of these compounds is an approved anti-aging treatment — speak to a doctor before taking any supplement or medication.
