Genes & Pathways

Key genes and signaling pathways that regulate aging.

DiscoveriesGenes & PathwaysMoleculesTechnologiesAging Topics
Pathway

mTOR

A master growth regulator. Reducing its activity extends lifespan in yeast, worms, flies and mice.

Pathway

AMPK

The cell's energy sensor, activated by low energy and exercise; promotes repair and efficiency.

Gene family

Sirtuins (SIRT1–7)

NAD+-dependent enzymes involved in DNA repair, metabolism and stress resistance.

Pathway

IGF-1 / Insulin signaling

Reduced signaling is one of the most conserved lifespan-extending interventions.

Gene

FOXO3

A transcription factor with variants repeatedly linked to human longevity.

Gene

APOE

Its variants strongly influence Alzheimer's risk and are associated with longevity.

Gene

TP53

The "guardian of the genome" — balances cancer prevention against tissue aging.

Gene

TERT

Encodes telomerase, which maintains the protective caps on chromosomes.
