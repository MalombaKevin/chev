About us

Biochevin

An aging research hub connecting scientists, studies and discoveries — so the science of living longer, healthier lives reaches everyone.

Who we are

Add healthy years to every life.

Biochevin funds, connects and communicates aging research. We bring researchers, institutions and the public together around one goal: understanding why we age, and using that knowledge to keep people healthy for longer.

We believe aging is the single greatest risk factor for disease — and that understanding it is one of the most important scientific challenges of our time. We fund, connect and communicate research so that breakthroughs reach people everywhere, not just a few.

  • Open scienceData and findings shared as widely and quickly as possible.
  • Global representationStudies that reflect the diversity of people who age.
  • Rigour firstEvidence over hype, always.
  • Public understandingScience explained so anyone can follow it.

Founder

Building a home for the science of aging

Kevin Malomba founded Biochevin to bring the people, studies and ideas behind aging research into one place, and to explain that science in a way anyone can follow.

“Longer lives only matter if they are healthy ones. Biochevin exists to help the science get there faster.”

What Kevin is building

  • ResearchA single view of active studies, publications and progress across the field.
  • PioneersA directory of the scientists and institutions leading the work.
  • LearnPlain-language guides to the biology of aging.
